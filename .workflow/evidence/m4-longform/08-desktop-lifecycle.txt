Coverage limitations:
- The window and creator are real Electron processes, but the tray-host probe is substituted with true/false gdbus fixtures.
- The active-quit test loads the lifecycle module inside Electron with an active callback and stubs the dialog response, cleanup callback and final app.quit. It does not prove cleanup during actual model production.
- Notification instances and IPC validators are real; Notification.show/isSupported and click emission are substituted. Hash navigation reaches the real preload and renderer.
- Native tray icon/popup rendering, native notification service delivery/clicks, Windows/macOS, active production cleanup and sleep blocking are not visually/end-to-end verified.
- M3 must emit the player-waiting DOM event before the new-chapter path is reachable from the application UI.
